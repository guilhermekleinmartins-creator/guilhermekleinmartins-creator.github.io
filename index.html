Guilherme Klein Martins

I am an economist interested in macroeconomics, economic development, and political economy. I am currently a Professor of Economics at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), in Brazil, and Research Coordinator in Macroeconomics at the Research Center on Macroeconomics of Inequalities (MADE-USP, University of São Paulo). I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Massachusetts Amherst and the Livre-Docência in Economics from the University of São Paulo (FEA-USP). Before joining UFRJ, I was a Lecturer in Economics at the University of Leeds.

My work has been published in journals such as the Journal of Development Economics, Oxford Bulletin of Economics and Statistics, Cambridge Journal of Economics, Industrial and Corporate Change, and Review of Radical Political Economics. I have written op-eds for Project Syndicate, The Conversation, Phenomenal World, Aeon, Folha de S.Paulo, O Globo, O Estado de S. Paulo, Valor Econômico and Nexo, and have been interviewed or quoted by The Economist, the Financial Times, BBC Brasil, CNN Brasil and CNBC Brasil.
